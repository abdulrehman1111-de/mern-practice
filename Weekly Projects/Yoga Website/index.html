OUR STORY

Started with a mat, a park bench, and a few curious neighbours.

Shafiq began practicing yoga alone in the early morning quiet of his local park. A few regulars noticed, asked to join, and within months a loose circle of neighbours had turned into a standing routine — green shirts, shared mats, and a WhatsApp group that never really goes quiet.

There's no studio, no membership desk. Just a patch of grass, an instructor who still practices like a student, and a community that shows up for each other as much as for the poses.

THE PRACTICE

A few favourites from our morning circle

Every session mixes gentle stretches with a few grounding poses. Here's a small taste — explained the way we teach it, in both languages.

Core & balance

Boat Pose / NavaSana

بوٹ پوز

  • Strengthens the core and lower back
  • Builds balance and coordination
  • Improves posture over time

Caution: skip if you have a recent abdominal injury or lower-back strain.

Relaxation

Rocking Pose / Rocking Movement

راکنگ پوز

  • Gently massages the lower back
  • Loosens hip and lower-back tension
  • Calms the mind before rest

Caution: move slowly, avoid if you have acute lower-back pain.

Warm-up

Dynamic Arm Swings / Warm-up

بازوؤں کی سوئنگ

  • Mobilises shoulders and upper back
  • Improves blood flow before practice
  • Prepares the body for deeper stretches

Tip: keep movements controlled, not forceful.
